# 15 — Creators and Partnerships

Status: Phase 1 research draft — 27 Sep 2026
Scope: Part 18 (creators and influencers) and Part 19 (partnerships): who TechPlay could work with, in what formats, what each side gets, and what TechPlay must have in place before anyone says yes.

The PR-and-creators agent wrote its digital-PR file (14) and was stopped before this one. This file is written from the pages it fetched for partnerships, the community agent's Patreon and Discord data (13), and the competitor research (04).

## Executive summary

1. **FACT — TechPlay is not listed as a critic outlet on Metacritic, and no OpenCritic listing was found.** Metacritic's `/publication/techplay/` returned 404. OpenCritic's FAQ says it weighs a publication's "demonstrated commitment to critically reviewing games", reviews that are "generally high-quality, thoughtful, structured, and nuanced", its "reach, audience, and influence", and whether staff "conduct itself in a professional manner". TechPlay's reviews stopped on 8 Jul 2026 after 38 (02).
2. **FACT — Review-code access now runs through platforms TechPlay can join.** Steam's Curator Connect sends review copies to curators "with the right voice, personality, and audience" without emails or keys. Keymailer supports "press and creators". PressEngine and Terminals (which claims "more than 29,000 verified media, streamers and YouTube content creators") distribute keys for publishers. Steam keys are a free Steamworks service that developers may give "to press/influencers".
3. **FACT — GamesPress will not carry TechPlay's own press releases.** Its FAQ says it does not tend to publish releases from "games magazines, websites, streamers" (14). It is a source of news and contacts, not a distribution channel for TechPlay.
4. **FACT — Creator-owned outlets are the strongest small-media model in the sample.** Second Wind (launched 8 Nov 2023) had 498 thousand YouTube subscribers (Wikipedia, as of 15 Oct 2025) and 19,449 Patreon patrons, 10,605 paying (Patreon API). Friends Per Second, a games podcast, has run since June 2022 with 106 episodes at two a month (Podchaser).
5. **FACT — Creator marketplaces exist but are built for publishers and brands paying creators.** Lurkit offers creators "free game keys", "paid sponsorships" and creator programmes; Creator.co and Collabstr are brand–creator marketplaces. They are ways for TechPlay to find creators, not free partnerships.
6. **FACT — TechPlay has a regional identity it does not use.** Its About page says "Independent · Sarajevo". It has a Bosnia and Herzegovina studios page. The A1 Adria League calls itself "the home of esports & gaming in Adria".
7. **HYPOTHESIS — The realistic partnership currency for TechPlay is data and distribution, not money.** A creator gets a data point, a tool embed or a Discord audience; TechPlay gets a link, a mention and new members.
8. **RECOMMENDATION — Before outreach, build four missing prerequisites:** a press and partnerships page, a review and ethics policy page (the scoring page exists), an ownership and AI-use statement, and a restarted review cadence.

## 5. What creators respond to (OBSERVATION from the evidence)

- **A clear, true number.** Data points travel (06, 14).
- **Tools their audience can use** (a Taste Match link, an Analyzer result, a year-in-review card).
- **Membership mechanics they already use** (Discord roles tied to support tiers are standard on Patreon; 13).
- **Credit and a link.**
