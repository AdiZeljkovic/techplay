## Executive summary

1. FACT — The five "majors" in this group are two very different animals. GamingBolt and Insider Gaming are lean WordPress news machines (16 GamingBolt news items in 48 h; Insider Gaming runs "EXCLUSIVE"-labelled scoops, a Discord, a weekly podcast and a newsletter) with no game database, no accounts and no comments. TechRadar and Tom's Hardware are Future plc brands whose parent reported Google Search+Discover traffic down ~20 % YoY, sessions down 15 % and affiliate e-commerce revenue down 24 % in H1 2026, and is answering with a 200,000-member "Future+" login programme and a £51/yr Tom's Hardware Premium (Bench database). Wccftech sits between: a 20-person Vancouver-registered hardware+gaming site that leans on a Google "Preferred Source" badge, a daily digest newsletter and launch-day guide clusters (six Control Resonant guides on one day).
2. FACT — TechPlay's real product competitors are the trackers, and the tracker market is bigger and more crowded than the media market suggests: Backloggd shows 69.6 M logged plays, 373 K games, 41.4 M ratings, 4.84 M reviews and 842 K lists on its homepage today; it had 350 K users and 1.3 K patrons in Jan 2025 and still has no mobile app and no store imports (its own FAQ). HowLongToBeat is now "an IGN Entertainment Inc. brand" (Ziff Davis), adds ~354 users and ~24.5 K backlog entries every 48 h, imports Steam libraries and runs a daily guessing game. A ResetEra poll (n=246, Jun 2025) found 34 % use no tracker at all, 22 % Backloggd, 13 % a spreadsheet, 11 % HLTB.
3. FACT — Automatic multi-platform import is the feature every tracker review and every community thread asks for and almost nobody ships well: Backloggd has none; HLTB does Steam only; GG and Stash are manual; Gamery (iOS-only, paid) and Infinite Backlog (web) do PSN/Xbox/Steam; SavePoint charges $9/mo for Steam/Xbox/PSN imports. TechPlay already does Steam, PlayStation, Xbox, GOG and Epic for free. That is a real, evidenced gap — but TechPlay has 60 users, so the gap is in awareness, not features.
4. FACT — The 2025–2026 media wave is worker-owned and reader-funded: Aftermath (Nov 2023), Second Wind (Nov 2023), Rascal, Rogue (ex-Polygon, tiers from $7/mo, "no ads or sponsored content"), Respec (ex-Eurogamer/VG247, launched 9 Jul 2026, Patreon-funded), Game File (29 K+ subs, $10/mo, >$140 K/yr), Post Games (1,000+ paid at five months), People Make Games (3,000+ patrons), Giant Bomb (bought back from Fandom May 2025). The Game Business counted 349 journalists leaving the trade in 2026 and 1,674 specialist journalists lost since 2020. "Frosted Cereal" and a gaming "The Rundown" do not exist as gaming newsletters — searches returned nothing.
5. FACT — The tool/wiki tier monetises through membership and utility rather than page views: Game8 gates watchlist notifications, interactive-map pins, build planners and "Premium articles" behind a free account; Fextralife, Wowhead, OP.GG, TrueAchievements and ResetEra all sell ad-removal; SteamDB, PlayTracker, IsThereAnyDeal and PCGamingWiki run on Patreon; Deku Deals runs on affiliate links. Maxroll now belongs to IGN/Ziff Davis; Icy Veins to Enthusiast Gaming; OpenCritic to Valnet; Metacritic to Fandom.
6. RECOMMENDATION (evidence in §8) — TechPlay should not try to be a sixth GamingBolt. Its defensible wedge is the one thing the evidence says is wanted and unshipped: a free, automatic, cross-store library that *reads back* something (hours, completion rate, taste similarity) — which is already what the live homepage promises. The cheapest acquisition moves visible in this dataset are (a) the utility page patterns of HLTB/SteamDB/Deku Deals (public, indexable per-game utility pages that work logged-out), (b) the "share-card" mechanics of Steam Replay/Backloggd year-in-review, (c) the launch-day guide cluster that Wccftech, Game8 and Deltia's run, and (d) a Discord+weekly podcast/newsletter loop like Insider Gaming's — all of which TechPlay's stack (Next.js ISR, Discord bot, 333 K-row catalogue) can already serve.
7. GAP — I could not measure traffic for any competitor; the only audited numbers are Future plc's (public company) and Press Gazette's UK panel figures. Everything else is self-claimed or third-party.

- Registration: required to log; browsing free. Monetisation: Patreon only (plus Google consent-mode ad tags present in HTML — ads likely for guests, UNVERIFIED).
- Vulnerabilities: no imports, no app (both on the roadmap for years), single developer, IGDB-dependent (Twitch/Amazon can change API terms), covers/data cannot be edited locally.
- TechPlay should learn: public logged-out game pages with review snippets; weighted ratings; edition merging; a public roadmap/changelog; the Goodreads "replaying" model TechPlay already has. Must not copy: manual-only logging as the core loop.
