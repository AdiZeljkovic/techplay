- **Q1 2027 is decided in Q4 2026.** Four datasets have to start logging now, because none can be backfilled: release-date changes (D-033, from about 25 Oct), launch prices (D-042, from about 26 Oct), wishlist price history (D-027, from 25 Nov) and the Next Fest participant list (ED list in October, D-043 capture in February).
